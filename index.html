Fill in a PDF online — free, private and simple

Use FillThisPDF to complete a PDF form directly in your browser. No signup, trial, payment card or watermark. Your original PDF stays on your device.

  1. Choose a PDF from your device.
  2. Fill existing form fields or place text, dates and checkmarks on the PDF.
  3. Download your completed PDF and reopen it in your PDF reader.

What can I do with FillThisPDF?

Fill interactive PDF forms, or write on regular and scanned PDFs by placing text on the page. Add checkmarks or a simple typed signature, undo edits, and download the finished file. It does not erase existing content, edit scanned text, or create certified digital signatures.

For personal documents, remember to download your finished file before closing the tab. Nothing is uploaded for manual editing.

Frequently asked questions

Is FillThisPDF really free?

Yes. You can fill and download a PDF without a subscription, trial, account, payment card or watermark.

Is my document uploaded to a server?

No. Manual PDF editing and exporting happen in your browser. Your file is not uploaded for the free editor. Avoid entering real personal information in the sample form.

Can I fill a scanned or non-fillable PDF?

You can place new text, dates or checkmarks on the page, including scanned PDFs. This does not recognize, remove or replace text already printed in the document.

Will the completed PDF open in Adobe Acrobat?

The download is a standard PDF, and the export is tested for Acrobat compatibility. Some password-protected, XFA or unusually structured files may not be supported.

Can I add a signature to a PDF?

You can add a typed name or signature-style text. This is not a certified digital signature or identity verification.
